## Sources reviewed but not reused

- [Official Waikiki Dental Facebook page](https://www.facebook.com/waikikidentalroseville/): verified by matching the practice address, phone, email, and website. Five older reels were visible, but they were not embedded because they are dated, vertically watermarked, and would add substantial page weight.
- [Google Maps listing](https://www.google.com/maps/place/Waikiki+Dental+%7C+Roseville,+CA/@38.7717804,-121.3154872,17z/): the anonymous gallery exposed a contributor-owned 2020 photo and warned that the image may be copyrighted. It was excluded.
- [Yelp listing](https://www.yelp.com/biz/waikiki-dental-roseville): linked by the legacy practice website, but the gallery is reviewer-uploaded and anonymous access was blocked by Yelp's device check. No Yelp media was copied.
- [Legacy Waikiki Dental website](https://waikikidental.com/): confirmed the Facebook and Yelp profiles and the same Roseville identity. Existing doctor and patient photography was already self-hosted in the redesign.

## Editorial boundary

Only media published by the practice itself was selected. Reviewer uploads, Google contributor photos, patient treatment images, and media with unclear ownership or consent were excluded.

Publishing an image on the practice's official social account verifies its
origin, but does not by itself document a transferable website license or each
person's consent. Before connecting the public custom domain, the practice
should confirm that it owns or controls these images and approves their website
use. Until then, do not reuse them in paid advertising, downloadable collateral,
or third-party channels.

## Future media intake

For new photography or video, retain the original master outside the Git repo,
record the capture date and photographer, confirm releases for recognizable
people, and create responsive first-party derivatives for the website. Prefer
brief, natural footage of arrival, reception, the team greeting a visitor, and
the care environment; avoid identifiable patient treatment unless explicit,
documented approval covers that use.
